# Backgrounds

Every Omarchy theme comes with its own backgrounds. Pick between them with `Super + Ctrl + Space`, or cycle to the next one with _Style > Next Background_ in the Omarchy menu. The background opens out of the middle of every monitor in Omarchy v4's slanted reveal; `"backgroundTransition": "none"` in your settings makes the change instant.

The selector shows every theme's backgrounds, not just the current theme's, plus your own under _Mine_.

### Your own backgrounds

Drop images into `Pictures\Wallpapers` and they show up under _Mine_. To use other folders too, list them in your settings:

```json
{ "backgroundDirs": ["~\Pictures\Wallpapers", "D:\Art"] }
```

The folders are indexed again after every login (no downloads), so new images appear by themselves.

### The lock screen

The background you pick is used for the lock screen too. Uninstalling puts your old wallpaper and lock screen back.
