# Commercial apps/services

_Install > Service_ in the Omarchy menu has the everyday apps and services that aren't open source: 1Password, Bitwarden, Dropbox, Spotify, Signal, Tailscale and NordVPN.

Some of them have a key of their own once installed: `Super + Shift + M` for Spotify, `Super + Shift + /` for 1Password, and `Super + Shift + G` for Signal (see [hotkeys](07-hotkeys.md)).

_Install > AI_ has Claude Desktop and Perplexity (see [AI](17-ai.md)), and _Install > Gaming_ has Steam, Battle.net, Minecraft and GeForce NOW (see [gaming](26-gaming.md)).
