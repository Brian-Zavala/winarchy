# Text Extraction & Dictation

### Text extraction

`Super + Shift + Print` lets you snip a region of the screen, and the text in it is copied to your clipboard, ready to paste. It works on anything: a screenshot of code, a photo of a document, a video frame. It's also on _Trigger > Capture > Text_ in the Omarchy menu.

The text is read by Windows' own text recognition, so nothing leaves your PC.

### Dictation

`Shift + F9` or `Super + Ctrl + X` starts dictation: talk, and it types into whatever has focus. Press it again to stop. A microphone shows in the bar while it's listening.

It's Windows' own voice typing, so it works in every app and every language Windows offers. Click the microphone in the bar to open the speech settings.
