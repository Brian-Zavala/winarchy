# Welcome to Winarchy!

Winarchy brings [Omarchy](https://omarchy.org)'s look, keys and themes to Windows 11. Omarchy is DHH's [omakase](https://manuals.omamix.org/3/omacom/76/omakase-computing) Linux setup on Arch and Hyprland; Winarchy is the closest thing you can get without leaving Windows: tiling windows, the Omarchy top bar and menu, the twenty-two Omarchy themes that recolor everything at once, the background and theme pickers, the screensaver, and the whole `Super`-key workflow.

It's built on [GlazeWM](https://github.com/glzr-io/glazewm) for tiling, [Zebar](https://github.com/glzr-io/zebar) for the bar and menu, [Flow Launcher](https://www.flowlauncher.com) for the launcher, [AutoHotkey](https://www.autohotkey.com) for the keys, and [Herdr](https://herdr.dev) for the terminal workspace Omarchy Quattro uses instead of tmux. Winarchy installs and configures all of it in one command, and takes it all back out in one command.

It also follows what Omarchy 4 (Quattro) added: the menu with its software catalog, Herdr and the AI coding-agent workflow built on it, and the agent usage panel in the bar.

Winarchy is unofficial. It isn't affiliated with Omarchy or 37signals, and it tracks Omarchy's releases rather than leading them. Where Windows can't do what Hyprland does, this manual says so.

Winarchy is for when you can't leave Windows yet, for work, a game or an app that only runs here. It's meant as a way in: learn the keys and the workflow here, and when you're ready, [install the real Omarchy](https://omarchy.org). Everything you learned carries over.

`Super` is the Windows key. Let's get started with the basics.
